# Motion

The header that follows the scroll, and the comparer's pills and text columns that slide together. Source paths (`routes/`, `components/`, `lib/`, `styles/`, `server/`, `app.tsx`, `document.tsx`) are under `web/src/`; other paths are relative to `web/`.

## The header that follows the scroll

`site-header.tsx` wraps the header in a slot and hands both to `followHeader` (`lib/header-follow.ts`, rules in `lib/bar-scroll.ts`, tested in `bar-scroll.test.ts`; in its place at the top of the page, else slid away on a step down and back in whole on a step up). The header moves by `transform` only (`data-mode` free, pinned or hidden), never by `top`, so showing and hiding it makes no layout shift; the slot keeps its height. Nothing is measured while hydrating: the first `ResizeObserver` callback sizes the slot and starts it, because a forced layout there tripped `HOT_SCOPE_TIME` in dev. A hidden header is `inert`, not `visibility: hidden`. A header that becomes `pinned` or `hidden` goes through `enter` in `header-follow.ts`, which starts its slide from where it is on screen: a free header scrolls with the page, so a bare `data-mode` change would run the transition from its place in the page rather than from where it shows. An element that sticks under the header reads `headerOffset.shown` (`styles/header-offset.stylex.ts`: the header's height while pinned, else 0) in its `top`, as the panel in `doc-layout.tsx` does, and carries `data-under-header`: when the value changes, `followHeader` measures how far each marked element moved and slides it back from there by `translate` with the header's own transition (its `CSSTransition` timing, none under reduced motion), so it moves with the header and only the stuck ones move. Prove a change with a per-frame record of the header's bottom and each follower's top.

## Pills and text columns

The pills and the text columns below the bar trade places as one: `createTextSlide` (`lib/text-slide.ts`, geometry in `lib/column-shift.ts`) writes each text column's shift, faintness and glide on `<html>` (`styles/column-shift.stylex.ts`, `data-cmp-sliding` marks it), and the text cells read them through `slidingColumn` (`styles/column-slide.ts`). A held pill is locked to the line (`createReorder` writes `--drag-x`, which the pill reads in its `translate`, not its `transform`, so its lift `scale` does not magnify the drag; `limitPill` bounds it) and its column follows what the pill shows, scaled from the pills' pitch to the text's. Every pill that is not held follows the rendered text column of its version on `translate` each frame while the session lasts (`followColumns`: the column's offset minus the layout move that `createReorder`'s live reorder made), so the pill and its column share offset, moment and easing; the other column fades as they cross. A reduced-motion reader gets the same poses without a glide (`poseColumns`). The drop, the keys and the swap link wait `GLIDE_MS` before they navigate, and the new `?v=` order clears the shifts and the pills' `translate`. Prove a change with a per-frame record of each pill's and each column's x (mouse drag, swap link, keys; two and three columns).
