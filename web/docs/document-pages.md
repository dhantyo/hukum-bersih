# Document pages

The layout of law, draft document and collection pages, and the folds in their text.
Source paths (`routes/`, `components/`, `lib/`, `styles/`, `server/`, `app.tsx`, `document.tsx`) are under `web/src/`; other paths are relative to `web/`.

## Document pages

Law, draft document and collection pages render through `components/doc-layout.tsx`: breadcrumbs, title block, a side panel (`panel` prop) and the text. It owns the panel: a ui-sdk `Resizable` group (panel, handle, text) up to the `media.tablet` breakpoint, and a ui-sdk `Drawer` from a tab on the left edge below it. The reader drags the handle (arrow keys work too) between the bounds in `lib/panel-size.ts` (`PANEL_REM`); dragging below the minimum, Enter on the handle or the "Sembunyikan" button folds the panel to a strip, and the same restore it. Whether it is folded is the `panel` preference (`data-panel` on `<html>`), or `comparePanel` while a comparison folds it below 1500px; the group follows the preference and writes it back. The width is `hb-panel-width` in `localStorage`; `WIDTH_SCRIPT` (inline, before the group) sets `--resizable-panel-size` for first paint, because the HTML is cached at the edge and `autoSaveId` restores only after hydration. The panel and the drawer's list scroll in the same ui-sdk `ScrollArea` (`PanelScroll`: the viewport is its `<aside>`, with a thin bar shown on hover; the drawer keeps its title bar outside it); without JavaScript it is not height-capped or sticky, because the area hides the native scrollbar in the server HTML. Below the breakpoint the group is forced to `display: block` (it writes `display: flex` inline), so without JavaScript the panel stacks above the text. The "Aa" settings sit in `site-header.tsx`; a page hands its text to them with the `text` prop, through `lib/reader-view.ts`. Breakpoints are the `media` constants in `tokens.stylex.ts`.

## Folds in the law text

BAB, Bagian, Paragraf and Pasal fold with a native `<details open>` (`components/law-fold.tsx`), so the text reads without JavaScript, find-in-page and `#pasal-N` open the fold, and no per-node script runs; ui-sdk `Collapsible` per fold measured slower on `/uu/11/2020/`. One `hashchange` listener opens the fold of a link's target. A part of a fold reads the fold's open state with `stylex.when.ancestor(":not([open])", fold)` and a dedicated marker from `styles/markers.stylex.ts` (never the default marker, which is on `<html>`). That is exact only for a visible part, because a part under a closed ancestor is hidden. A style that is set only under a condition needs a real default: `default: null` in a later style removes the earlier value.

## Collapsibles

A fold opens and closes at once; only its chevron turns (`collapse.turn`, `collapse.triggerArrow` in `styles/collapse.ts`, duration and easing `collapseDuration` and `collapseEasing` in `styles/motion.stylex.ts`, none under reduced motion). The spacing around a trigger never depends on its state: margins, paddings and borders of a summary and of the fold sit on the fold and its summary unconditionally, never under `:not([open])` or `[open]`, so the trigger stays where it is when it opens or closes; the body adds only its own inner spacing, which goes with it when it closes. A ui-sdk `Collapsible` panel takes `collapse.panel` (its `--collapsible-panel-height`, `data-starting-style`, `data-ending-style`), which animates its height with the measuring ui-sdk provides. "Tutup semua" and "Buka semua" hold the fold under the top edge in place (`keepInView` in `lib/fold-motion.ts`). A fold whose open state is decided after hydration (the Pembukaan) is rendered with `data-seed`, which `styles/stylex.css` hides (scripting on, not the original format) until the effect sets `open` and removes the mark, so the first paint is the hydrated one. `html` keeps `scrollbar-gutter: stable` so a page that shrinks does not change width.
